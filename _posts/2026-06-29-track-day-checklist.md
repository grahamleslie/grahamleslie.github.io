If you aren't keeping a logbook at the track, you're just burning gas. 

Consistency in performance driving comes down to managing your variables. The most volatile of those is tire pressure. Especially if you’re running a limited camber setup (like -2° on a BMW M235i), your logbook is what keeps you from destroying your shoulders in a single afternoon.

One thing you learn quickly: trash tires like the Firehawk Indy 500s will lie to you with mushy sidewalls and inconsistent deltas. A high-performance 200TW tire like the Yokohama AD09 requires a disciplined, aggressive baseline to stay structural. 

Here is the functional baseline for AD09s, followed by the raw data from my sessions at Sonoma and Laguna Seca.

### The Baseline: Yokohama AD09 Specs

#### Autocross (Protect the Sidewall)
Common mistake is starting too low. You need initial stiffness to keep the tire from rolling over before it builds heat.
*   **Cold Start:** 33+ PSI Front / 31 PSI Rear (Start high to support the sidewall for Run 1)
*   **Hot Target:** 37-39 PSI Front / 35-36 PSI Rear
*   **Abort Zone:** 41 PSI or 165°F

#### Track Day (Sustained Heat)
Don't get cute with low cold starts. If you start too low, you'll scrub the shoulders before you even reach the hot window.
*   **Cold Start:** 30 PSI Front / 28 PSI Rear (Minimum)
*   **Hot Target:** 36-37 PSI Front / 34-35 PSI Rear
*   **Safety Limits:** Fronts **must** stay above 35 PSI Hot to prevent rollover. Max temp: 170°F.

### Measurement Rules
1.  **The Chalk Test:** Mark the sidewall triangle. If the scrub passes it, add 2 PSI.
2.  **Heat Management:** If tires hit 170°F, **do not bleed air**. If you bleed air when the tire is overheated, you lose the structural support the tire needs as it recovers. Pit and cool down instead.
3.  **Pressure Ceiling:** If you're sliding at 40 PSI, you've reached the limit of what air pressure can fix.

### Track Logs: Real-World Deltas

#### Sonoma Raceway (09/20/25)
*   **Session 1 (60° Ambient):** Start 29/31 or higher for initial stiffness. 
*   **Session 3 (70° Ambient):** Ambient rose to 70°. Hit 37/37 Hot. Bleed -2/-2 to stay in the window.
*   **Note:** If the front starts to feel mushy, you've bled too much.

#### Laguna Seca (12/01/24)
*   **Session 4 (66° Ambient):** Finally hit 34-36 Hot.
*   **Observation:** Temps hit 170°F. The window for these tires is 125-150°F.
*   **Correction:** Need to alternate hot/cool laps. Constant scrubbing in the corkscrew is killing the RHS (which consistently runs 2° hotter).

### Why This Matters
The scratch pad doesn't have to be pretty. It just has to be accurate. When you look back at these logs before your next event, you aren't guessing—you're looking at the data that saved your sidewalls.

**Simple, but Significant.**
