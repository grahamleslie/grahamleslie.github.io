If I'm not keeping a logbook at the track, I'm just burning gas. 

Consistency in my driving usually comes down to managing variables, and the most volatile of those is tire pressure. Especially since I'm running a limited camber setup (-2°) on my M235i, the logbook is what keeps me from destroying my shoulders in a single afternoon.

I've learned the hard way that tires like the Firehawk Indy 500s will lie to me with mushy sidewalls and inconsistent deltas. Moving to a 200TW tire like the Yokohama AD09 required a much more disciplined, aggressive baseline to keep the tire structural.

Here is the functional baseline I've settled on for my car, followed by the raw data from my last few sessions at Sonoma and Laguna Seca.

### My Baseline: Yokohama AD09s

#### Autocross (Protecting the Sidewall)
I used to start too low, thinking I'd build into the pressure. Now I start higher to keep the tire from rolling over on Run 1 before I have any real heat.
*   **Cold Start:** 33+ PSI Front / 31 PSI Rear
*   **Hot Target:** 37-39 PSI Front / 35-36 PSI Rear
*   **Abort Zone:** 41 PSI or 165°F

#### Track Day (Managing Sustained Heat)
I don't get cute with low cold starts anymore. If I start too low, I'm scrubbing the shoulders before I even hit the window.
*   **Cold Start:** 30 PSI Front / 28 PSI Rear (Minimum)
*   **Hot Target:** 36-37 PSI Front / 34-35 PSI Rear
*   **Safety Limits:** I make sure my fronts stay above 35 PSI Hot to prevent rollover. If I hit 170°F, I stop.

### My Ground Rules
1.  **The Chalk Test:** I mark the sidewall triangle. If the scrub passes it, I add 2 PSI immediately.
2.  **Heat Management:** If I hit 170°F, **I do not bleed air**. Bleeding air on an overheated tire just kills the structural support it needs as it recovers. I pit and cool down instead.
3.  **Pressure Ceiling:** If I'm sliding at 40 PSI, I've reached the limit of what air pressure can fix for my setup.

### Track Logs: Real-World Deltas

#### Sonoma Raceway (09/20/25)
*   **Session 1 (60° Ambient):** I started at 29/31 for initial stiffness. 
*   **Session 3 (70° Ambient):** Ambient rose to 70°. I hit 37/37 Hot and had to bleed -2/-2 to stay in the window.
*   **Note:** If the front starts to feel mushy, I know I've bled too much.

#### Laguna Seca (12/01/24)
*   **Session 4 (66° Ambient):** Finally hit my 34-36 Hot target.
*   **Observation:** Temps hit 170°F (The window for these is 125-150°F).
*   **Correction:** I need to alternate hot/cool laps. Constant scrubbing in the corkscrew kills my RHS, which consistently runs 2° hotter than the left.

### Why I Do This
My scratch pad is never pretty, but it's accurate. When I'm looking back at these logs before my next event, I'm not guessing at my starting pressures—I'm looking at the data that saved my sidewalls.

**Simple, but Significant.**
